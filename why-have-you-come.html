Why Have You Come?

A Word from the Editor

Nobody walks up Champion's Way by accident. Somebody's owed, somebody's dead, or somebody's lying, and it's often you. Work out which, and bring the answer to your first session. The Editor would print your story if he could. Give him one worth printing.

In Short

Choose one of the five backgrounds written for this city, then answer the questions below about how your character is tied to Drakkenheim and the people around them. Bring your answers to your first session.

Five Lives That Lead Here

Continental NobilitySome noble house in Caspia, Elyria, or Westemär had the misfortune of producing you. The Fall either ruined your family or handed it an opening, and you're here about one or the other. Remember that no one with arcane magic in the blood may hold a title. Your cousins certainly remember.Last of the Line: the only one left, without a copper to your name, come to dig the family back out of the rubble. Possibly by hand.
Ask YourselfWhich side did your house back in the war, and who still holds that against you?
MagebornMagic came in with your blood, and the Amethyst Academy came for you not long after. You're out from under them now, but never out from under the laws that bind every mage in the realms.Malfeasant Mage: expelled or self-taught, and very careful about who finds out which.
Ask YourselfWhat did the Academy take from you when it took you in, and would they have you back?
Devoted MissionaryYour faith sent you. Or a vision did. Or a prophecy you couldn't shake, however much you drank.Stargazer: you watched the star fall from a long way off, and something's had a hook in you ever since. Some nights you can feel it pull.
Ask YourselfWhat sacred purpose brought you here, and would the village chapel bless it?
SurvivorYou stayed when everybody with sense ran, and you know which streets will kill you, because you've watched them do it.Veteran of the Civil War: soldier or refugee, you walked away from the war. Not all of you did.
Ask YourselfWhat did you lose in those ruins, and what did you have to do to get out?
Treasure SeekerGold brought you, or relics, and you'd just as soon not say which. The Courier respects your privacy and will be watching you anyway.
Ask YourselfWhat is the one prize worth dying for, and who else wants it?

Questions for Anyone

  • The FallWhere were you the night the stars fell, and who did you know in the city?
  • The WarDid you march on the capital in 1112, or fight for Mannfred or Cecilia? What followed you home?
  • The BannersWhich of the five factions would you trust, and which would you never serve? Does anyone you love wear their colors?
  • The WorldWhich realm do you call home, and what do they say about you there? What faith do you keep, if any?
  • The Road BackIf you don't come back, who should be told?
  • Each OtherDo you know someone else on this road? You might be kin, share a teacher, be old rivals, owe them your life, or have seen their face in a dream.
