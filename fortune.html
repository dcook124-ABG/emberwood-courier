    The Market Report

    A Fortune in the Rubble

    Opportunities for the brave and the gloved.

    Drakkenheim was about as rich as cities get, and the night it died almost nobody got their valuables out. There are vaults sealed under fallen towers, silver still in the drawers of noble townhouses, and spellbooks on the shelves of mages who won't be back for them. All of it sits in the Haze, the lawful property of people too dead to object.

    Then there's the stone. Delerium grows through the ruins, fused into wall and street, humming to itself and drinking up magic. Mages will pay damn near anything for it, which makes it the most valuable thing in Westemär and the worst thing to be found holding.

    Trades in stones and stranger wares out of a carriage the size of a chapel in Caravan Court, and pays best to those he likes. Get liked.

    Orson Fairweather

    Buys delerium and doesn't care where you got it, from an iron-bound stagecoach ringed with armed men who'd as soon you didn't linger.
