    What Is Waiting Past the Gate

    The Dark City

    Read this before you go in. Nobody reads it after.

    A hundred thousand souls lived in Drakkenheim. What lives there now is the Haze, the stones, and whatever the pair of them have made of the leavings. The scavengers have hammered fifteen years of dying down to five rules, and you'll notice no one who broke them has come forward to complain.

    The Rules of the Road

    1. No rest in the Haze.Sleep in there and you'll get up as tired and torn as you lay down. Come out to heal, or don't come out.
    2. Never touch the stones bare-handed.Gloves. Tongs. A rag if you're down to it. Those are your fingers, and you'll want them for counting the money.
    3. Know your way out before you go in.Retreat is a plan. Running blind is a funeral.
    4. Not every monster is meant for you.There are things up there would eat your whole crew and pick their teeth with the pack frames. Go around.
    5. Mind your company.Five outfits work the ruins. Every one is hiring, and every one keeps a tally of who it owes and who owes it.

    What the Survivors Say

    Dangers of the Dark City

    The Haze

    A mist of every color there is rolled through the streets the night of the Fall and has not had the decency to leave. It sits on the whole city and slops out past the walls. It does things to sleep and to spells, and worse to whoever stops too long, and fifteen years of clever bastards have failed to keep it out of anything.

    The Stones

    Delerium comes up through the ruins like a rash, fused into brick and cobble. It hums. It shines after dark, so you'll find it, and it'll sicken you if you handle it the way you've handled everything else in your life. Bring tools.

    The Change

    Stop too long or get careless with the stones and the ruins go to work on you, meat first and then mind. Your charms against poison and plague are so much jewelry, and sleep won't touch it. Only magic turns it back. If your friend comes home wrong, you do not wait to see if it passes.

    The Things Inside

    The dead up there don't all keep still, and what used to be dogs and rats quit that years ago. Most of what'll come at you down those streets once paid taxes.

    The Gates and the Walls

    Five gates into the Inner City. The Hooded Lanterns hold one and talk big about the other four. Ask a scavenger who's holding the rest and watch the sudden interest they take in the bottom of their cup. As for going over the walls, you're welcome to try it. Once.

    The People

    Plenty up there have nothing against you personally. They just want what's on your back. A crew staggering out heavy is a sight easier to rob than a vault is to find, a sum the Queen's Men worked out years ago.
