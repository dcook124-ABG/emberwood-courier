Who's Hiring in the Ruins

The Five Banners

Five powers, one dead city, and not one of them with bodies to spare.

Compiled by Odile Fenwick, Political Desk, who has been threatened by all five

In Short

Five factions are fighting over the ruins: the Hooded Lanterns, the Queen's Men, the Knights of the Silver Order, the Followers of the Falling Fire, and the Amethyst Academy. All of them hire adventurers, and each keeps a contact in Emberwood. Working for one changes how the others treat you.

Every one of these outfits knows exactly what Drakkenheim is for. They simply disagree, loudly and with steel, and not one of them has the bodies to win the argument alone. That's where you come in. Each of them will tell you they're the good ones. The Courier reports. You decide. Tap a crest and meet the people who'd like you to bleed for them.

The Hooded Lanterns

Lord Commander Elias Drexel

“One gate in three years. We'll take the other four. Sign on and help us be quicker about it.”A sergeant at Shepherd's Gate
Who They Are
What's left of the old City Watch, plus whatever the civil war didn't finish off, in green cloaks with yellow trim. On paper they're the 4th Provisional Expeditionary Force to Reclaim the Capital. Nobody has ever said the whole name twice without stopping for a drink.
What They Want
The capital rebuilt, the kingdom stitched back together, and a rightful arse on the throne. Several noble houses are paying the bills, each of them quietly hoping the arse turns out to be a cousin.
Bear in Mind
They count scavengers among the vermin to be cleared out of the city. Make yourself useful before they get around to you.
Where to Find Them
Ansom and Petra Lang, at the Lantern Watchtower.

The Queen's Men

The Queen of Thieves

“Everything in that city belongs to somebody now. Mostly us.”A smiling stranger, Skull & Sword
Who They Are
A hundred gangs of brigands, smugglers, and cutthroats, every one paying tribute to a woman no two witnesses describe the same way. Tall. Short. Young. Old. Scarred. Beautiful. She signs her orders with the Queen of Diamonds.
What They Want
A criminal empire, and a city kept exactly as lawless as it is this minute.
Bear in Mind
If you find a note pinned to that card, you do what it says. That is not the Courier's opinion. It is the Courier's policy.
Where to Find Them
Blackjack Mel, at the back tables of the Skull & Sword.

Knights of the Silver Order

Knight-Captain Theodore Marshal

“The stone is not treasure. It is rot that learned to shine.”A knight on the Emberwood road
Who They Are
Paladins and oathsworn knights of the Sacred Flame, marched up from Elyria at the word of the Divine Matriarch, in silver plate and clean white tabards. In this mud. Every single morning. Nobody has worked out how.
What They Want
Every crystal smashed, every monster put down, and the Falling Fire heresy stamped flat.
Bear in Mind
The finest manners you'll meet this side of the grave. If you cast spells, they'll be watching your hands the whole time they're being polite.
Where to Find Them
High Flamekeeper Ophelia Reed, at the Chapel of Saint Ardenna.

Followers of the Falling Fire

Lucretia Mathias

“The star fell for a reason. Come and see it, and you'll understand.”A pilgrim on Champion's Way
Who They Are
Pilgrims by the hundred, farmers and laborers mostly, in sackcloth, walking toward the Crater behind a former Flamekeeper the Faith has named a heretic. They sing as they go. You'll hear them before you see them.
What They Want
Every faithful soul delivered to the Crater and the whole ruin made holy ground. They say the stone is a gift from on high, sent against a darkness that hasn't arrived yet. That's the cheerful part.
Bear in Mind
Those who finish the road take part in a rite at the Crater's Edge. The Flamekeepers have a word for it, and the word is blasphemy.
Where to Find Them
Nathaniel Flint, at Hendrix Farm.

The Amethyst Academy

Archwizard Eldrick Runeweaver

“Bring it to us in a lead box and we pay in gold. Bring it in your pocket and we pay for the funeral.”Overheard at the Red Lion Hotel
Who They Are
Part school for mages, part guild with its fist closed round the whole trade in magic. They keep precious few of their own people in the field and pay the likes of you to take the risk instead. They'll tell you it isn't cowardice. It's procurement.
What They Want
The stone studied and put to work, and their great stronghold, the Inscrutable Tower, handed back to them.
Bear in Mind
Polite, punctual, and they've never once told an outsider one thing more than was asked. They'll pay you. They won't shake your hand.
Where to Find Them
The Academy's buyer, at the Red Lion Hotel.

Before You Take Anybody's Coin

Who's at Whose Throat

Sign with one and you've made up your mind about the other four, whether you meant to or not. Tap a crest.

Courier Advises: You can work for more than one banner. Plenty have. Just never drink with two of them in the same room.

  • The Hooded Lanterns and The Queen's MenThe Queen's people sabotage whatever the Lanterns build, and the Lanterns hang whatever they catch.
  • The Hooded Lanterns and Knights of the Silver OrderBoth want the monsters dead. The knights say nothing gets rebuilt till every stone is ash. The Lanterns would like to live there afterward.
  • The Hooded Lanterns and Followers of the Falling FireOne side wants a capital and the other a holy ruin, and the pilgrims keep wandering, singing, through the middle of the Lanterns' war.
  • The Hooded Lanterns and The Amethyst AcademyCivil, for now. The Lanterns would dearly like to know what the mages want with their city.
  • The Queen's Men and Knights of the Silver OrderThe Order would hang every last one of them given a free afternoon. The Queen takes care never to give them one.
  • The Queen's Men and Followers of the Falling FirePilgrims carry everything they own and won't fight for it. The gangs thank the Flame for them daily.
  • The Queen's Men and The Amethyst AcademyThe mages pay top coin for stones and never ask whose they were. The Queen's people know it.
  • Knights of the Silver Order and Followers of the Falling FireHeretics, says the Order, and it means to see their prophet tried. The pilgrims pray for the knights, which does not help.
  • Knights of the Silver Order and The Amethyst AcademyCenturies of burned mages lie between them, and only a treaty keeps it from starting again. The knights want the stone destroyed. The mages want it by the cartload.
  • Followers of the Falling Fire and The Amethyst AcademyTo the pilgrims the stone is holy. The Academy grinds it up for trinkets.
